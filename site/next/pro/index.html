Questions

When do the boards update?

MLB rebuilds four times a day — early morning, late morning, midday and before the night games — with fresh prices each time. NFL and college boards build daily through the week and sharpen as kickoff gets closer. Golf and NASCAR post for each event and refresh as the forecast comes into range.

Whose prices are these?

The sportsbooks'. We pull US books' lines and prices and show the median (or the best price, where noted). They're never our numbers, and they move — always check your book before you bet.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your account. Your desks stay open through the end of the period you paid for.

What's All-Access?

DFS Kitchen All-Access ($60/mo) bundles DFSRADAR PRO with DFSPREP, DFS Books and The Optimizer. If you have it, log in with the same account — PRO is already included.

Is this betting advice?

No. It's information: forecasts, history and our own numbers, with every sample size shown so you can judge them. Tendencies aren't guarantees — the record shows the misses too. 21+. If gambling is a problem for you, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
