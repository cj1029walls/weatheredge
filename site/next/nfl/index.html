How to read this page

Wind is measured against the field. A crosswind punishes kickers and deep passing far more than wind blowing straight down the field, where each team gets it at its back for half the game. The small diagram on each card shows the angle.

Similar weather means games at the same stadium within a few degrees and a few mph of the kickoff forecast, since 2015. Over/under shares grade each of those games against its own closing total.

Forecasts refresh every morning and sharpen as kickoff gets closer. Domes and closed roofs are weather-neutral.
