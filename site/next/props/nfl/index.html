How a weather lean is made

A lean needs two things: a forecast strong enough to matter (15+ mph wind, or a sub-40° kickoff) and a player whose own games in that weather moved his number — quarterbacks down 8%+ in wind over 5+ games, receivers down 10%+, backs up 5%+ when the wind pushes teams to run.

The split, its sample and the forecast behind it are on every row. Small samples are shown so you can judge them.

What "the books' number" means

Lines and prices are the sportsbooks' own — the median across US books at our last pull, never ours. Books post player props a few days out; leans can appear before their prices do.
