How our HR number is built

Each hitter starts from his own home run rate over three seasons, then we adjust for tonight: the weather at that park (from every game there since 2015), the plate umpire's history, the opposing starter's HR/9 and the park itself.

That model number is blended 60/40 with the market's own price — the market knows about lineups and injuries we don't. Where we still disagree by 5+ points on a priced prop at +150 or longer, with at least three books posting, it earns a value flag.

What "the books' number" means

Prices are the sportsbooks' own numbers, pulled through the day — the median across US books unless a best price is shown. They're never ours, and they move.

Every prop is graded at the price posted when we published it. A hit at +400 is +4.00 units; a miss is −1.
