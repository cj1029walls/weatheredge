How to read this page

The four numbers on each card are the forecast at first pitch: temperature, wind (with a diagram of the field — the arrow shows where the wind is blowing), rain chance, and how home runs have run at that park in games played in weather like today's, against the park's own normal.

Similar weather means games at the same park within a few degrees and a few mph of today's forecast, since 2015. Every number shows how many games it's built on — small samples are shown, but treat them as small.

Wind-sensitive parks are the ones where wind has measurably moved home runs over the years. At a wind-shielded park, the same breeze matters less.

Forecasts refresh through the day — morning, late morning, midday and before the night games. A roof-closed game is weather-neutral no matter what's happening outside.
