How a prop edge is built

1 · The forecast

At the venue, at game time

Temperature, wind against the field, rain and humidity for the hours that matter — refreshed through the day.

2 · Our number

Measured against history

What actually happened at that venue, for that player, in weather like it — with the umpire, park and matchup layered in. Samples shown, always.

3 · The books' number

Side by side, then graded

The sportsbooks' line and price beside ours. Every call is graded afterward at the price that was posted — wins and losses stay on the record.

No promises, just numbers. A prop edge is a disagreement with the market, not a guarantee — some weeks the books are right. That's why every sport's record is public, including the misses, and why our HR model's calibration (what we said vs what happened) sits on the record page.
