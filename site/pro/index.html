How the number is made

When lineups post (~4 PM ET), plate appearances follow the batting slot (leadoff ≈ 4.65, nine-hole ≈ 3.6) and benched players drop off the card; before lineups, everyone gets a flat 4.0. Each batter's three-season HR rate is shrunk toward the league average (small samples can't fake an edge), then adjusted by tonight's weather at the park (DFSRADAR's similar-conditions history), the plate umpire's HR tendency (shrunk, capped at ±12%), and the opposing starter's HR/9 (shrunk by innings). That rate over ~4 plate appearances gives the HR chance. Edge compares our chance to the books' median price with ~7 points of vig stripped out. When a live price exists, 25% of the market's implied probability is blended into ours (market anchoring). Career batter-vs-pitcher history joins the mix only at 10+ at-bats, shrunk hard and capped — a 1-for-6 shows as history, never as a multiplier. 2+ HR shows our multi-homer chance against the books' alternate line. A VALUE flag needs a 3-point edge, at least 3 books, and +150 or longer — at most five per night. K projections = shrunk K/9 × shrunk innings per start × the park's weather strikeout edge at half weight, leaned only when 0.8+ Ks off the line. Every night's card is archived and graded.

Odds via The Odds API · median of US sportsbooks · lines move — always check the current price.
