How the number is made

When lineups post (~4 PM ET), plate appearances follow the batting slot (leadoff ≈ 4.65, nine-hole ≈ 3.6) and benched players drop off the card; before lineups, everyone gets a flat 4.0. Each batter's three-season HR rate is shrunk toward the league average (small samples can't fake an edge), then adjusted by tonight's weather at the park (DFSRADAR's similar-conditions history), the plate umpire's HR tendency (shrunk, capped at ±12%), and the opposing starter's HR/9 (shrunk by innings). That rate over ~4 plate appearances gives the HR chance. Expected HRs per game is the sum of those individual probabilities. Edge compares our chance to the books' median price with ~7 points of vig stripped out; when a live price exists, 25% of the market's implied probability is blended into ours. Career batter-vs-pitcher history joins the mix only at 10+ at-bats, shrunk hard and capped. A VALUE flag needs a 3-point edge, at least 3 books, and +150 or longer — at most five per night. With-ump splits (how tonight's batters and starters have fared with this exact plate umpire, 2022–2026) are context only — those samples are far too small to move a probability, and we won't pretend otherwise. Game totals blend season offense, the opposing starter's shrunk ERA, the park's weather runs history, and the ump's runs tendency. K projections = shrunk K/9 × shrunk innings per start × the weather strikeout edge at half weight. Every night's card is archived and graded — the calibration table below is the receipts.

Odds via The Odds API · median of US sportsbooks · lines move — always check the current price.
