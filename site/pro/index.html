Each desk is built around the edge that actually exists in that sport — not the same template five times. Each desk's calls are archived before the games and graded from the final results in a public record, losing stretches included.

When desks update. MLB rebuilds four times a day — early morning, late morning, midday and before the night games — as lineups and prop lines post. NFL, CFB, PGA and NASCAR rebuild every morning as forecasts sharpen, and football gets a final pre-kickoff refresh on Saturday and Sunday. A desk reads Off-season when its sport has nothing scheduled and Delayed when its last rebuild didn't land — the desk itself says what's missing.
