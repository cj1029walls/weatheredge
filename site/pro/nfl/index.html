How the NFL board works

Built from 2015–2025 play-by-play and schedule data: referee crew tendencies (pts/gm, over rate, penalty volume), travel and rest spots with their real against-the-spread and over/under records, each team's scoring identity by weather band, and QB/kicker condition splits. When kickoffs enter the 8-day forecast window, weather-dependent leans activate (QB pass-yds in 15+ mph wind, kicker accuracy in wind and cold, team totals in sub-40°). When sportsbooks post player props, each lean carries the live line and price. Every lean is archived the moment it posts and graded publicly after the games — same receipts-first rules as the MLB board. Historical records are tendencies, not guarantees; sample sizes are shown on every number.

Lines & props via The Odds API · median of US sportsbooks · lines move — always check the current price.
History: nflverse schedule + play-by-play, 2015–2025 · forecasts: Open-Meteo.
