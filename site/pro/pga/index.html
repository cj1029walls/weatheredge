How the PGA board works

Player results for every PGA Tour event since 2018, with every finish expressed as a field percentile (winning a 156-man open ≠ 20th of 30 at East Lake). Course history ranks players by their shrunk average percentile at this exact event — small samples get pulled toward the field median so a one-start fluke can't top the board. Wind pedigree rebuilds past event-week weather from the archive, tags each edition windy or calm (12+ mph average daytime wind), and measures who holds up when it blows — those leans activate only when this week's forecast is windy. Wave splits from the free radar carry forward so the draw edge sits on the same card. Every lean is archived the moment it posts and graded against the final leaderboard. Historical records are tendencies, not guarantees; sample sizes are shown on every number.

Results: ESPN public scoreboard, 2018–present · event-week weather: Open-Meteo archive · forecasts: Open-Meteo.
